A pocket sound engineer for the Behringer XR18

XR18 Studio is a free app to control your XR18, X18 or MR18. An assistant guides you step by step to make your band sound good. The console is complete, X-AIR Edit style. Each musician adjusts their in-ear mix on their own phone.

⬇ Free download 📘 Beginner tutorial GitHub

Windows · macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) · Linux — no account, no subscription, no Internet needed

For bands without a sound engineer

Rehearsals, small venues, bars, parties: you have a Behringer XR18, mics and musicians, and you want a good sound without learning the jargon.

🎧 “Mon ingé son”

It sets up each instrument with live best practice, sets the gains with you and prepares the monitor mixes.

🎚️ A clear console

Every track visible at once. Simple mode for the show, full X-AIR Edit style Expert mode.

📱 Monitors on phones

Each musician scans their QR code and adjusts their own in-ear mix. There is no app to install.

🎧 “Mon ingé son”: a soundcheck in 6 steps

Nothing changes without your OK: every change goes through a before/after window, a backup is made, and one button undoes it all.

  1. ConnectThe XR18 is found automatically, like in X-AIR Edit.
  2. Who is plugged in whereVocals, guitars, bass, detailed drums (kick, snare, 3 toms, 2 overheads)… Each instrument gets its complete setting: high-pass, EQ, compressor, gate, stereo.
  3. Set the gainEach musician plays loud. The coach measures, proposes the right gain and fits compressor and gate to your real signal.
  4. Listen and fix“It’s muddy”, “it squeals”… Say what bothers you and it suggests a small correction.
  5. Prepare monitorsA pre-fader mix per musician based on their role, then QR codes in one click.
  6. Keep the good settingThe whole mix is saved as a scene, ready for the next show.

🎚️ The whole console at a glance

  • Every track on screen, no scrolling: 16 inputs, Aux and effect returns.
  • The X-AIR Edit views: Mixer, Channel, Input, Gate, EQ, Comp, Sends, Main, Effects, Routing, Meters.
  • Every control explained in plain words (in French), with all 61 effects described.
  • Start from a blank scene in one click, without touching 48 V, gains or routing.
  • Always protected: 48 V, routing and effects ask for confirmation, with a backup.

📱 Each musician’s in-ear mix, on their phone

  • A personal QR code per musician, scanned with the phone camera.
  • Only their own mix: each instrument level, volume, mute, saved settings.
  • Ears protected: capped volume, rises in small steps.
  • Lost link? A new one is created in one click and the old one stops working.

FAQ

Does XR18 Studio replace X-AIR Edit?

It offers the same views (Mixer, Channel, Input, Gate, EQ, Comp, Sends, Main, Effects, Routing, Meters). It adds a beginner assistant and monitors on phones. You can use both on the same console.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source (MIT licence).

Does it work with an X18 or MR18?

Yes: XR18, X18, MR18 and their V2 versions are detected automatically.

Do musicians need to install an app?

No. Each one scans their QR code and adjusts their mix in their phone browser, on the same Wi-Fi.

Is Internet needed?

No. Everything runs on the console’s or the band’s local network.

Does the coach hear the sound?

No. It reads the console’s settings and levels, not the audio. Its listening advice comes from your description. Proposed values are starting points: your ears have the final say.

Is the app in English?

The interface is in French. The guides and the tutorial are available in English, with every French button name translated.
